**Common definitions.** The accounting and model window is June 2022–November 2025 (42 months). The denominator of every cap-gap point is $764.7482532227002 billion. A positive paired marginal is cumulative reported response-off cash principal minus response-on cash principal. Only the conserved, once-scaled closed-book comparison equates that difference with additional total principal retained; monthly rescaled reporting does not identify such a stock. It is not a positive response-on minus response-off runoff difference. Unless varied, simulated scenarios use the saved Freddie sample, imported 6.5% quarterly response convention, 4.991% annual floor, hard maximum, 100 PSA, full moving share, realized rates, and observed-holdings flow reporting. Both response legs change together when a scenario changes an input.
