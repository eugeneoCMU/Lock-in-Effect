## 2. Separate the next identification work from sensitivity runs

| Question | Minimum new information and design | Paper treatment until complete |
|---|---|---|
| What fraction of voluntary prepayment reflects a home sale? | Loan-level termination reasons or a defensible linkage to property sales, with clear date and population coverage. Estimate the fraction by rate-gap and relevant vintage/credit cells, then rerun the response on that measured component. | Keep the 25/50/100% bracket labeled as assumed; do not infer the share from current payoff flags. |
| How much is housing supply rather than financing lock-in? | Local inventory/listing and transaction data linked by geography and month to the loan risk set; a design that can compare rate-gap variation while holding local supply and borrower composition credibly fixed. Specify the counterfactual and pretrend/overlap checks before estimating. | Keep the housing-activity correction as a transport sensitivity. Do not call the off-window floor a control group. |
| Do calibrated borrower covariates matter? | Re-estimate the credit, LTV, and burnout terms on a defined pre-QT estimation sample, or pre-specify a simpler model that removes them. Rerun paired legs on the same loans and seeds and compare the marginal, level fit, and holdout performance. | Call the current coefficients calibrated priors and retain their sensitivity disclosure. |

These are distinct projects. The Freddie composition rerun can proceed with the available raw files; the sale-share and housing-supply estimates require additional data and identification work. The covariate exercise needs a fixed estimation/holdout split and should be planned before reading its outcome.
