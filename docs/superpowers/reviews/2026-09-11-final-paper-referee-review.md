# Cold Referee Review: Final Paper Narrative Pass

## Decision

Ready for circulation and for a journal-specific formatting pass. I find no submission blocker in the rebuilt 34-page journal main. The paper now states a narrower and defensible contribution: most of the cap-relative accounting gap was anticipated, while mortgage lock-in added a positive, economically meaningful, model-based cumulative marginal whose magnitude depends on sampling, calibration, coefficient transport, and functional form.

## Referee questions

- **Is the contribution clear by page 2?** Yes. By the end of page 2, the paper has separated the observed cap-relative gap from the allocation-dependent New York Fed comparison, posed the portfolio-level question, and introduced the paired response-on/response-off translation. The explicit contribution paragraph continues on page 3, but the research object and design are already clear.
- **Can a reader distinguish observed, published, imported, calibrated, and simulated quantities?** Yes. The abstract and Introduction label the cap gap as observed accounting, the New York Fed path as a published allocation-dependent comparison, the moving response as imported, the turnover floor and combination rule as calibrated, and the marginal and subgroup allocations as simulated or model-based.
- **Does the paper lead with a range while preserving the anchor?** Yes. The abstract, Introduction, Results, long abstract, and poster all lead with the $+1.9$ to $+9.1$ production-form sampling interval and then identify $+5.6$ points or $42.6 billion as an interior anchor.
- **Is functional form treated as part of the result?** Yes. The calibration box and approximately $+11.2$-point additive construction are presented as separate assumption layers without a confidence-interval interpretation.
- **Does the paper avoid claiming monthly validation?** Yes. It explicitly reports the failure of discriminating monthly-path tests and confines the supported result to cumulative trapped principal.
- **Does every main-text robustness result change interpretation?** Yes. The section retains turnover-floor transport, functional-form and moving-share uncertainty, conventional-agency replication and coverage limits, the adverse alternative-model comparison, and the monthly-validation failure. Run provenance and seed-level implementation detail remain in the online appendix.
- **Does the conclusion answer the research question directly?** Yes. It states the observed gap, the model-based sampling interval and anchor, the wider assumption sensitivity, the identification boundary, and the cap-design implication in five short paragraphs.

## Remaining evidentiary limits

- The $87.8 billion expectations-relative comparison depends on within-year allocation, may already include some lock-in, and uses the spring-2022 rate path.
- The production-form interval propagates sampling uncertainty in the floor read at a fixed imported response; it is not total model uncertainty.
- The floor-and-elasticity box and additive result are assumption sensitivity without statistical coverage.
- The imported moving response is applied to all voluntary prepayment in production, while the moving share is not estimated.
- Direct loan-level coverage is conventional and incomplete for Ginnie, excluded vintages, and fifteen-year collateral.
- Borrower, cohort, and forgone-move results are model-based allocations, not observed moves, causal heterogeneous effects, or welfare estimates.
- The cumulative decomposition survives; monthly timing does not.
