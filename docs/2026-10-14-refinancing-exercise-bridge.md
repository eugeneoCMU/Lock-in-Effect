# Refinancing exercise bridge: implementation and evidence boundary

The existing supplied-quote borrower solver now has a separate stochastic exercise interface. No accepted manuscript result or production simulation has been changed.

At each decision node, export `A = wait_value - exercise_value` per dollar of opening principal. Both branches account for supplied fees at their applicable refinancing dates and all future exercise opportunities; waiting does not charge today's fee unconditionally. `A > epsilon` means the supplied solver prefers immediate exercise; equal branch costs select waiting. Static savings alone do not establish this gate.

The monthly refinance intensity is `lambda_refi = alpha * eligible * 1[A > epsilon]`. Binary eligibility is supplied explicitly. Alpha is a conditional exercise rate, not a separately identified attention rate, qualification probability or processing-delay parameter. It remains an assumption until fitted on a documented complete cause-labeled outstanding-loan risk set.

This is a two-stage reduced-form overlay. The original dynamic solver assumes immediate future exercise availability; alpha does not enter its value recursion. That recursion also omits forced sale/default exits; the supplied competing intensities do not feed back into its branch advantage. Fitting the overlay therefore does not solve an optimal policy under delayed opportunities. A future jointly optimized delay model would need to include the exercise-arrival process in the Bellman recursion. The present approximation also freezes incentives, eligibility and cause intensities during the month, even though the borrower solver compares closing immediately with waiting one month.

Let Lambda be the sum of four monthly cause intensities: refinance, sale-induced mortgage termination, other termination and default. For each first-exit cause j, `p_j = lambda_j/Lambda * (1 - exp(-Lambda))`; survival is `exp(-Lambda)`. At zero total intensity all cause probabilities are zero and survival is one. Sale mobility can enter the separately supplied sale intensity; a quarterly household mobility response is not an estimated monthly refinance response.

Calibration maximizes the complete-cause multinomial likelihood on borrower counts. Each homogeneous risk cell contains one month of opening exposure and mutually exclusive outcome counts summing exactly to that exposure. Do not substitute balance weights. Supply five provenance descriptions, complete label coverage, a comparable opening-loan risk set and refinance scope matching same balance and remaining term. These declarations make the assumptions inspectable; software cannot certify source labels.

The fit filters the calendar before accessing outcome fields and hashes only the training contract/cells. Later outcomes cannot influence the estimated alpha. Duplicate cell/month keys, fractional counts, incomplete causes, unknown-payoff categories and model-impossible training events are rejected. No eligible exercise exposure returns `not_identified`. Positive exposure with zero refis returns `zero_boundary`. Reaching the numerical upper search boundary returns `upper_boundary` without a usable estimate. Held-out structurally unsupported causes are retained and reported; rounded probability underflow does not create unsupported events, because likelihoods use stable log probabilities; the log score is explicitly unavailable in that case.

Usage: solve with `solve_dynamic_refinancing`, extract with `dynamic_incentive`, convert with `bridge_probabilities`; estimate from a complete-cause panel using `fit_exercise_rate`, then score later cells with `evaluate_exercise_rate`. The repeatable demonstration is `tools/run_refinancing_bridge_demo.py --output <new directory>` and creates fixed-seed synthetic data, never observed borrower results.

Real-data readiness remains unresolved. The audited Freddie files give voluntary loan payoff events without a general sale/refi distinction. The audited NSMO public fields give termination timing but no cause or actual dollar closing costs. The linked 2024 ASMB form does not contain the claimed realized cause split. Fannie HARP supplies selected historical positive refinance links; matching objectives and a complete comparable risk set are still unestablished. These are dispositions from the saved source audit, not newly checked online data availability.

The next data task is to establish a cause-labeled pilot and document its denominator, program eligibility, coverage, censoring, competing exits and refinance objective. Until then, there is no empirical alpha or revised portfolio dollar result from this bridge. Simultaneous Dunn–Spatt lender valuation and endogenous par quotes remain absent.
