## Priority 1: State the question and its limits

1. Lead with the $764.748B observed cap gap, the $87.8B central expectations comparison, and the paired response-on minus response-off estimand. Keep these three objects separate in the abstract, introduction, Table 1, and conclusion.
2. Call $42.6B / +5.6 points an interior production-specification anchor. Place the conditional floor-read interval beside it, then foreground the larger floor, elasticity, functional-form, and moving-share sensitivities. Do not give those assumption ranges a confidence-interval interpretation.
3. Put the unestimated moving share and its 25/50/100% bracket in Table 1. Explain that the imported moving response is applied to all voluntary prepayment in production.
4. Keep housing supply and baseline-turnover transport beside the central result. Treat the hard maximum versus additive rule as an economic model choice.

## Priority 2: Bound the external claim

5. State that the Freddie 2017-2021 direct sample covers about 51% of SOMA face. Preserve the separate Fannie, Ginnie, older-vintage, and 15-year checks without describing them as direct measurement of the full book.
6. Treat the $87.8B comparison as allocation- and rate-path-dependent; it is not a clean no-lock-in or surprise estimate.
7. Keep monthly timing, borrower-group causal effects, household welfare, and the Danish mechanism outside the identified portfolio result.

## Priority 3: Presentation and next research

8. Use a title and abstract that identify MBS runoff and the model-based decomposition. Describe the policy implication as publishing an achievable cash-flow range alongside the operational cap.
9. Retain the current short-paper/Online-Appendix split. Make only changes that improve the argument or clarify a number; move detailed derivations into the appendix when necessary.
10. Future empirical work should discipline the moving share using home-sale versus other voluntary terminations and separate housing-supply changes from financing lock-in. These are research extensions, not checks claimed by this revision.
