# Cotality payoff-data inquiry

Status: drafted, not submitted. Contact route: [Payoff Analysis contact form](https://www.cotality.com/products/payoff-analysis#rc-form). The form requires business email, phone, organization, title, country, and privacy-policy consent.

## Message for the contact form

I am conducting academic research on mortgage lock-in and Federal Reserve agency-MBS runoff. I am looking for a historical research extract that distinguishes a voluntary mortgage payoff associated with a property sale from a refinance or other payoff. I saw that Cotality Payoff Analysis reports these categories.

Could you tell me whether a loan-level research extract is available for June 2022 through November 2025, including both paid-off loans and the comparable outstanding loan risk set? I would need payoff month and reason, origination date, original note rate or coupon, loan type/term, and outstanding balance or a way to construct it. Please also indicate coverage and match rates by month, how sale-related payoffs are classified and validated, whether the data can be used in a published academic paper, and the approximate cost or academic licensing route. A sample schema and coverage table would be very helpful. A de-identified extract is sufficient.

Thank you,
Eugene Ong

## Research decision

Continue using the existing conditional-simulation manuscript while the inquiry is pending. Do not replace the moving-share scenarios with a point estimate until the denominator, match coverage, and external validity of any proposed data are assessed.
