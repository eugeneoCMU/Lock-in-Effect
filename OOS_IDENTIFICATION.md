## Verdict

The lock-in marginal is SIGN-ROBUST out of sample: it stays strictly positive at every floor x elasticity cell (confirmed), and the temporal holdout — floor calibrated on Jun2022-Dec2023, marginal summed over the strictly held-out Jan2024-Nov2025 months — is +$45.1B (vs +$44.8B at the production 4% floor over the same months), so the positive sign is not an artifact of the floor touching the evaluation months. On MAGNITUDE: anchored on the CLEAN out-of-window involuntary floor (the 2018 rising-rate leg, ~5.0% CPR, age>=12, refi-suppressed), the central (6.5) marginal is ~$43B (range $33-$52B across the clean floor band), materially BELOW the $70B in-sample production point but NOT collapsed to the benchmark-consistent ~$16.6B. The collapse to ~$6B occurs ONLY if the contaminated, refi-inflated pooled/2019 floor (~6.9%) is used — and that read is disqualified as a pure involuntary floor because its turnover rises with loan age (seasoning contamination) and sits in a falling-rate refi wave (regime contamination). A SINGLE OOS floor is NOT cleanly identifiable — only a range — because (a) 2017-2019 lacks deep-OTM MATURE cohorts (the clean 2018 leg has NO age>=24 OTM cohort-months at all), (b) the pooled and 2019 reads are refi-contaminated, and (c) consequently the decisive mature-age seasoning test is NOT COMPUTABLE on the clean leg — 2018 is clean by rate-regime construction, not clean by test, and its one computable age contrast ([0,12)->[12,24)) is confounded by PSA-ramp immaturity. The reported OOS range DOES contain the $16.6B benchmark-consistent figure at its contaminated-floor edge.

### Anchors NOT computable from available data

- **The clean leg's decisive seasoning test cannot be run.** The 2018 rising-rate leg has **zero** age≥24 out-of-the-money cohort-months — loans that are out-of-the-money when rates *rise* are all recently-originated low-coupon loans, hence young. So the mature-age involuntary probe (age≥24 vs [12,24)) is **NOT COMPUTABLE** on the clean leg: 2018 is clean by rate-regime construction, not clean by test. Its one computable age contrast ([0,12)→[12,24)) is confounded by PSA-ramp immaturity and is reported as diagnostic context only. This is precisely why the out-of-sample floor resolves to a **range, not a point**.
- Deep out-of-the-money selection comparable to 2023–24 (gap ≤ −0.02) is **not available** in 2017–2019: rates peaked near 4.94%, so no cohort reaches that discount depth. The out-of-window grid therefore stops at gap ≤ −0.005.
