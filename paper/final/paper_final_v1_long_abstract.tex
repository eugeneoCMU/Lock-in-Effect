\title{Mortgage Lock-In and Federal Reserve MBS Runoff: A Model-Based Decomposition}
\author{Eugene Ong}
\affil{Carnegie Mellon University}
\date{}
\begin{document}

\begin{abstract}
\noindent From June 2022 through November 2025, Federal Reserve agency-MBS caps exceeded the net decline in holdings by \$764.7 billion. The cap is not a no-lock-in counterfactual: allocating the New York Fed's May 2022 projection gives an expectations-relative gap of \$87.8--\$186.8 billion. I combine SOMA holdings, Freddie Mac composition, an imported moving response, and a turnover floor in paired response-on and response-off simulations. At the hard-maximum specification, assumed moving shares of $0/25/50/100\%$ yield $0/+1.8/+3.4/+5.6$ points of the cap gap. The \$42.6 billion full-share result is not an empirically preferred estimate. At the same 4.991\% annual floor, a 75--150 PSA sweep spans $+0.9$ to $+15.6$ points; the survival-combination form gives $+11.2$ at 100 PSA. Scaling the opening book once reduces the original marginal to \$37.0 billion. Supported-cell results expand their pool to total holdings and do not measure the supported slice's actual contribution. In a frozen retrospective check, response-on predictions overstate voluntary prepaid principal by 23\% and perform worse than a simple pre-evaluation cohort-speed baseline. The moving share is unestimated, portfolio transport is incomplete, and these conditional, model-based comparisons are not a common confidence interval or a newly identified causal effect. Cap and cash-flow projections should distinguish interest-rate scenarios from prepayment assumptions. The model's 4.991\% turnover floor is transported from 2018 discount cohorts; it is not a measured purely involuntary hazard. Floor-read sampling uncertainty maps to $+1.9$ to $+9.1$ points at the central imported response and maximum form, separately from PSA, moving-share, accounting, and population sensitivity. The fresh draw contains 75,000 unique May-2022 Freddie survivors across twelve supported UMBS cells, representing 47.6\% of SOMA face under its assumed bridge. Changing the bridge from 60 to 100 basis points produces $+4.83$ to $+5.92$ points with the same IDs and targets. These data and specification checks improve transparency but do not validate the imported elasticity or supply an untouched evaluation sample.
\end{abstract}
