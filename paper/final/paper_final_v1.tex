\begin{abstract}
\noindent From June 2022 through November 2025, agency-MBS caps exceeded the net holdings decline by \$764.7 billion. This accounting gap is not a causal lock-in estimate; allocating the published May 2022 projection gives an expectations-relative gap of \$87.8--\$186.8 billion. A completed twenty-quarter Freddie reconstruction restricts the simulation frame to original 360-month fixed-rate mortgages and audits chronology, eligibility, cohort histories, competing terminations and principal accounting. With inherited parameters, the corrected hard-maximum benchmark gives \$16.2 billion in reported OFF-minus-ON principal, or \$14.3 billion in closed-pool cash. The historical \$42.6 billion calculation remains version specific. Sequential substitutions show the largest change when pre-May burnout and fixed-cohort denominator initialization replace legacy initialization; they do not identify a pure parser effect. Mixtures assuming 75\% sensitivity and an illustrative positive-gap response give \$49.6 and \$57.6 billion under inherited and matched-OFF references. Neither share nor reference is estimated. On 4,998 previously examined cohort-months, both mixtures improve error relative to the hard-maximum and additive ON benchmarks, but remain worse than the simple cohort-speed baseline. Fresh floor measurement inference is reported separately from structural sensitivity. Missing early histories remain unknown. Imported-response validity, floor transport, sale/refinance classification and SOMA representativeness remain unresolved; computational acceptance does not establish a preferred amount, total uncertainty or causal identification.
\end{abstract}
