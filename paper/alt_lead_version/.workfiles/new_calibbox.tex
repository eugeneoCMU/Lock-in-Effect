\subsection{Floor and Elasticity-Band Sensitivity: The Calibration Box}\label{sec:robustness-floor}

\subsubsection{The calibration box}

Path B's dominant level-setter is the baseline turnover floor, whose 4\% calibration Section~\ref{sec:pathb} concedes is measured inside the evaluation window. I swept it across seven values from 2\% to 6\% annual CPR, jointly with the full Liebersohn--Rothstein elasticity band (5.5\%, 6.5\%, 7.7\%), rerunning the central and $\beta_1 = 0$ null legs at every floor under the production convention: same committed 75,000-loan sample, same seed and regime tuple, one shared macro frame. Both sweeps were pre-committed --- grid, parity gates, and interpretive thresholds entered the repository before any run executed (commit ordering in the replication package's ledger). The fresh runs reproduce the committed artifacts exactly at the production point: central \$818.530 billion, null \$748.185 billion, marginal $+\$70.345$ billion, floor-bind share 36\% of 1,683,124 evaluated loan-months.

Appendix~\ref{app:floormech} collects the sweep mechanics and the contamination separation behind the off-window anchors. Their exhibits sit in Section~\ref{sec:hazard}: the calibration box (Table~\ref{tab:floorband}), the marginal as a curve in the elasticity (Table~\ref{tab:lowband}), and the out-of-sample anchors (Table~\ref{tab:oosfloor}).

\subsubsection{Level versus form}

The box sweeps the floor's level, never its functional form: the choice that censors the elasticity. A form test specified ex ante reran both legs with \eqref{eq:pathB}'s hard maximum replaced by a competing-risks combination on the survival scale, $h^{\mathrm{prep}} = 1 - (1 - \underline{h})(1 - h^{\mathrm{vol}})$, under which the elasticity is never inert; the max-form pair reproducing the committed artifacts exactly was its parity gate. The marginal moves to $+11.25$ points at the production floor, a $+2.05$-point shift past the $\pm 1$-point immateriality threshold fixed ex ante. The form-dependence is real. Under the additive form it is nearly floor-invariant, $+11.29$ to $+11.21$ points across the 3--5\% floors whose max-form span ($+10.8$ to $+5.5$) failed Appendix~\ref{app:floormech}'s stability threshold: that gate measured the max form's censoring mechanics, not instability in the elasticity's contribution.

The aggregate level is strongly form-dependent: the additive central leg recovers 67.2\% at the production floor against 107.0\% under the max form, a 4\% involuntary hazard added to moderate voluntary hazards raising book-wide prepayment. The max form stays production for its semantics, a minimum on \emph{total} turnover matching the observed 4--5\% on cohorts aged 12--24 months in the depth plateau carrying 61.5\% of gap-zero exposure --- the reading the deep-discount calibration anchors, and where the two forms nearly coincide. That comparison is context for the level, not the selection rule for the marginal's form, since the design does not identify levels. At the off-window anchors (pre-committed run \texttt{floor\_form\_offwindow}) the additive form returns $+11.22$/$+11.21$/$+11.19$ points at the 4.695/4.991/5.334\% floors, within 0.06 points of its production-floor value: the max-form marginal's whole in-window-to-off-window demotion runs through censoring, and the near-coincidence defense does not reach the shallow-gap off-window anchors.

The form dimension is a measured curve, not a two-point hull (run \texttt{floor\_form\_mixture}; both endpoints reproduce the committed forms bit-exactly). Let $\omega$ be the share of the measured floor entering as an additive competing involuntary cause (a different object from Path A's stratum index $s$) and $1-\omega$ a floor on total turnover: $\omega = 0$ is the production hard maximum, $\omega = 1$ the additive form. Off-window the marginal is $+7.5$ points by $\omega = 0.1$, $+9.7$ at $\omega = 0.25$, $+10.7$ at $\omega = 0.4$, flat near $+11.2$ from $\omega = 0.6$ up (peak $+11.24$ at $\omega = 0.8$, a 0.03-point plateau dip to the additive endpoint), while central-leg standalone-scorer recovery falls from 100.4\% to 55.9\%. So Section~\ref{sec:pathb}'s reading that the strictly-involuntary share is plausibly well under half does \emph{not} pull the marginal toward the max end: modest additive shares already close most of the gap, an additive component acting wherever the max form censors. The headline stays at $\omega = 0$ on the max form's semantics --- the conservative endpoint of a measured curve, not a position defended by fit.

The level cost carries off-window, large enough to report beside the hull: at the 4.991\% headline anchor the additive central leg recovers 55.9\% of the benchmark against a 44.7\% null, 59.3\% and 52.0\% at the 4.695\% and 5.334\% anchors at the central band point, 43.5 to 45.3 points under the max form's aggregate level throughout. The hull's upper endpoint is its least accurate cell --- $+13.10$ points, additive at the 4.695\% floor and the band's 7.7\% high edge, its central leg recovering 61.2\% of the benchmark it is scored against --- so the form-conditional range spans specifications differing sharply in aggregate fit, and its upper end should not be read as an equally credentialed member.

Form and transform, reported side by side and never crossed, were presumed to add; the pre-committed joint cell (\texttt{concave\_additive\_marginal}) shows they do not. Concave under the additive form puts the marginal at $+9.25$ points at the production floor and $+9.22$ at the 4.991\% anchor --- inside the hull at both, so the range stands --- but the interaction is $-1.47$ points at the headline anchor, past the same $\pm 1$-point convention. Censoring is why: the transform costs $-0.52$ points under the max form, which absorbs most of it into the floor, against $-1.99$ under the additive form, which never censors. A successor run closes that grid (run \texttt{concave\_hull\_band\_ends}): sixteen more off-window cells take the form $\times$ transform product to thirty-six of thirty-six, and the hull does not survive intact. Seventeen of the eighteen concave cells fall inside it, but concave under the max form at the highest defensible floor and the band's low edge returns $+3.53$ points, below the $+3.89$ lower edge the log-linear cells set, widening the hull at the bottom to $+3.5$ to $+13.1$ points; the upper edge is unchanged, the concave transform being signed downward with its highest cell at $+10.83$. Composing form and transform adjustments additively is unsupported at the calibration this paper headlines. The form-robust statement of the identified quantity is a lock-in marginal of roughly $+9$ to $+11$ points at the production floor, positive under every level, band, and form combination tested.

\paragraph{A transportable lesson: the floor's functional form is a first-order choice.} The involuntary-turnover floor is calibrated, not estimated --- a nuisance parameter by construction, there to keep ordinary turnover from being attributed to the rate gap. Its combination rule is nevertheless first-order for the reported marginal, and the leverage is largest exactly where this paper takes its headline: at the off-window anchor the switch carries the marginal from $+5.6$ to $+11.2$ points, a doubling, against a rise from $+9.2$ to $+11.2$ points --- a little over a fifth --- at the in-window calibration. What transports is the curve's shape, rising steeply then plateauing: reporting the two endpoints alone understates the exposure of any design that calibrates a floor, a censoring threshold, or a competing risk and then differences across it. The 2018 ladder carries own-data evidence on the same fork, out-of-window and out-of-episode. Re-binning its nested reads by exposure-weighted differencing gives per-bin turnover of 7.09\% and 6.99\% in the two shallowest bins, then 4.67\% and 4.65\% in the two that follow --- a step, then a plateau (run \texttt{depth\_ladder\_shape}). A floor on total turnover censors the voluntary hazard once the gap is deep enough, so per-bin turnover should stop falling; an additive competing-risks form never censors and predicts continued decline. The plateau is what the max form predicts. Two qualifications: the ladder does eventually fall, to 4.35\% and 3.61\% in the deepest bins, but those hold 3.85\% and 0.02\% of exposure and the source artifact's own rule flags them as not well supported, as it does the bin between them, which \emph{rises} to 5.14\% rather than falling and so is not evidence of continued decline either; and this is a shape comparison on one leg, not a test --- I read it as own-data evidence consistent with the production form, not as a resolution of the fork.

\subsubsection{The off-window re-anchor}

Re-running both legs with the floor at each defensible off-window value, production convention otherwise, gives Table~\ref{tab:oosfloor}. Across the 2018 leg's defensible band the marginal runs $+6.8$ to $+4.3$ points ($+\$51.8$ to $+\$32.6$ billion), centering at $+5.6$ points ($+\$42.6$ billion) at that leg's mid-grid 4.99\% reading; jointly with the full Liebersohn--Rothstein band, $+3.9$ to $+7.5$ points. The small numbers come from the contaminated anchors: the pooled 6.07\% read, at the box's adjacent 6.0\% row, gives $+2.3$ points, the 2019 leg's 6.91\% read $+0.7$. An off-window floor exceeds the in-window 4\% and the marginal decreases in the floor, so a higher anchor shrinks the identified contribution. It does not reverse it. The direction of the in-window concession stands, but not the conclusion that the plausible contribution sits at the box's lower edge: that edge was an artifact of the refinancing-contaminated pooled read, and the defensible off-window floor places the contribution in the box's interior, at roughly $+5.6$ points, not at $+2.1$ to $+2.4$. Every read in that band sits on cohort-months aged twelve to twenty-four, the only ages the 2018 leg contains, so its mature-seasoning test is not computable (Appendix~\ref{app:floormech}); uncomputable is not passed. Where the mature contrast is measurable it runs one way in every cell (Section~\ref{sec:identification}), putting the band below the mature book's baseline turnover and the marginal it implies above what that book would give.

The move from the in-window anchor to the off-window one changes two things at once; until now the paper charged both to the window. In-window reads 3.972\% at gap $\leq -2$ points, off-window 4.991\% at gap $\leq -0.25$ points; through the committed floor-to-marginal grid --- PCHIP on the frozen sweep, not re-derived --- the move is $-\$28.312$ billion, or $-3.702$ points. At matched depth it splits: window, holding depth at gap $\leq -0.25$ points on both sides, $-\$20.874$ billion ($-2.729$ points, 73.7\%); depth, taken within the in-window panel from gap $\leq -2$ points to gap $\leq -0.25$ points, $-\$7.438$ billion ($-0.973$ points, 26.3\%). Re-anchored at the deepest depth both panels support (gap $\leq -1$ point), 66.9\% window and 33.1\% depth. The window dominates either way, and the demotion is not primarily a depth artifact. But depth owns a real and previously unattributed \$7.4 billion, so the description must change: at matched depth the in-sample comparison point is $+\$63.5$ billion, not the $+\$70.3$ billion this paper narrates the demotion against, and roughly \$20.9 billion of the \$27.7 billion demotion is the window --- the rest a change in the discount depth at which the anchor is defined, with nothing to do with out-of-sampleness. The reverse ordering (window first at matched deep depth, then depth) is not computable: it would read the off-window leg at gap $\leq -2$ points, where that leg retains 0.02\% of its exposure. So no average of the two orderings is formed, and the depth-first path is reported alone. Extending the 2018 depth ladder from three well-supported depths to five leaves the committed clean band exactly [4.695\%, 5.334\%], the added 4.722\% at gap $\leq -0.75$ points and 4.869\% at gap $\leq -1$ point falling strictly inside; the marginal band stays \$33.31 to \$51.75 billion around the \$42.58 billion point, its \$33.31 billion grid-read and \$32.6 billion direct-run endpoints differing by the PCHIP grid-read wedge, committed maximum \$0.69 billion --- also the tolerance to which the grid reproduces the committed sweep throughout.

Two further pre-committed measurements discipline this band. First, the floor reads carry sampling error the depth cuts do not express. A 1{,}000-replicate stratum-cluster bootstrap puts the clean-band reads' standard errors at 0.39--0.46 points of CPR against 0.16--0.19 in-window: the 2018 leg rests on 31 clusters---that selection occupies 31 of the panel's 296 four-way strata---against 226. Propagating the point anchor's replicates through the committed grid gives a percentile 95\% interval of $+3.0$ to $+8.0$ points on the off-window marginal, double the depth-cut band's width, so the binding uncertainty on the headline marginal is the floor's sampling error, not the depth convention. That percentile read under-covers at that cluster count and is demoted; the pre-committed corrections (run \texttt{floor\_inference\_correction} and its Webb-weight successor \texttt{floor\_inference\_correction\_v2}, the first's percentile layer reproduced bit-exactly as its parity gate) widen it mostly at the top, and the corrected ladder with its degrees of freedom is Table~\ref{tab:ladder}. The restricted wild-cluster inversion's $+2.3$ to $+9.1$ points is the binding layer, its lower edge above zero on every corrected read, selected by run \texttt{fewcluster\_coverage}'s pre-committed coverage rule, with the Webb wild-$t$'s $+2.9$ to $+8.7$ beside it. Second, age-standardizing the off-window floor reweights the 2018 leg's age-bucket reads to the in-window deep-discount population's age mix and imputes its empty mature buckets from the in-window age gradient (84.0\% of weight imputed, and labeled as such). The standardized floor reads 5.51\%, above the clean band's top, for a measured marginal of $+3.7$ points against the $+3.8$ its grid position implies --- an indicative bound, not a measurement, but signed one way: the clean band is open below $+4.3$, and that endpoint is not a conservative floor.

That read holds the conventional accounting fixed. The Ginnie overlay of Section~\ref{sec:pathb} holds the floor at 4.991\%. Both correct the same $+5.6$-point cell and both cut the same way, but they do not look like the same adjustment counted twice: one moves the floor's level, the other confines the marginal to the conventional face share. The overlay's share scaling is identical across the three speed series it was tested on, so carrying that $0.797$ scaling up to the higher floor---a carry the overlay run does not itself establish---would put the marginal near $+3.0$ points, the bottom of that sampling interval and below the form-conditional hull's $+3.5$ lower end. The joint cell is now run (run \texttt{b5\_joint\_cell}) and returns $+2.9$ points, an interaction of $+0.0$ against the proportional projection. A composed point would not have been credible: the concave transform under the additive form, the first joint cell this paper ran for two adjustments to the marginal, returned an interaction of $-1.47$ points at the headline anchor.

The same matched-depth machinery undercuts the argument by which Appendix~\ref{app:floormech} rejected the 2019 leg, though not the rejection. That argument sets the 2019 leg's gap-$\leq$-0 read of 6.910\% against the retained 2018 leg's gap-$\leq$-0.25-point read of 4.991\%: a 1.919-point spread taken across two different depths, not a valid comparison as written. At matched depth the legs do not converge either --- over the four depths at which both clear the 10\% support rule the 2018-versus-2019 spread runs 1.576, 0.491, 2.112, and 0.857 points, non-monotone in depth, exceeding the mismatched 1.919 at half a point. So neither ``the legs disagree by 1.9 points'' nor ``the legs agree once depth is matched'' is a defensible summary; the second cherry-picks one depth in four. The pooled 2017--2019 read is not a third independent leg --- it contains both 2018 and 2019 (and 2017), so any appeal to three legs agreeing double-counts; there are two --- and the 2019 leg's own exposure collapses from \$1{,}085 billion at gap $\leq$ 0 to \$199 billion by half a point and \$0.29 billion by one and a half, making its deep reads noise on the grounds that disqualify the deep off-window cell. The honest verdict is that the refinancing-contamination story is unadjudicable below gap $\leq$ 0 rather than refuted there. The only depth carrying solid exposure for both legs is gap $\leq$ 0, where 2019 sits 1.576 points above 2018, directionally consistent with refinancing contamination and with the seasoning diagnostic that independently disqualifies the 2019 leg. The conclusion stands; the argument as earlier stated does not, and I state the weaker one.

What it leaves standing is a definitional alternative, not a measurement dispute. Suppose the floor's level is itself an equilibrium outcome of the episode: turnover depressed by the same gaps the elasticity prices, as the refi-era reads' higher levels would suggest under that reading. Then part of what the rate-inelastic baseline absorbs belongs to the lock-in channel, and the rate-inelastic share overstates what is invariant to household behavior. Pricing that channel requires a cross-regime turnover-versus-gap relationship estimated outside this window's rate configuration. This design does not contain one. I name it as future work. I do not fold an unestimated correction into the headline.

\subsubsection{Floor stability over months}

A second instrument addresses the months dimension, not the population one. It is a floor-stability check, not an outcome holdout: no realized prepayment from the held-out months enters either simulated leg, so it can falsify only the floor calibration's temporal stability, never the marginal against realized data. Calibrating the floor on deeply out-of-the-money turnover confined to the window's first nineteen months (June 2022 through December 2023) returns 3.97\%; the central-minus-null marginal then sums over the remaining twenty-three months (January 2024 through November 2025) alone, which enter that calibration nowhere. That held-out marginal is $+\$45.1$ billion, against $+\$44.8$ billion at the production floor over the identical months: very nearly the magnitude, and with it the sign, survives evaluation months the calibration never touches. The decomposition is exact because trapped liquidity is a monthly sum against the redemption target, which cancels from the central-minus-null difference, the monthly marginals adding back to the aggregate. Across every floor and elasticity-band combination run here---twenty-seven cells spanning floors from 3.97\% to 6.91\%---the marginal is positive without exception, a consistency check the window's rate configuration cannot let fail (Section~\ref{sec:identification}).

The identified content of the lock-in channel is therefore a magnitude that survives both instruments as a bounded interval: $+4.3$ to $+6.8$ points at the defensible off-window floor under the production max form, $+3.9$ to $+7.5$ jointly with the elasticity band, $+9.6$ to $+13.1$ under the additive form at the same anchors, and a form-conditional hull of $+3.5$ to $+13.1$ points. Against that, the in-window production calibration returns $+9.2$ points, now reported as this paper's in-sample calibration point, not its headline.
